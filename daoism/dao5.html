Heavenᵃ and Earth are indifferentᵇ, They treat the ten thousand things as straw dogsᶜ;¹ The sage is indifferent, He treats the people as straw dogs;² Is not the space between Heaven and Earth like a bellows? It is empty, yet does not collapse; It acts and more flows from it;³ Too much speech tends to exhaustᵈ itself;⁴ It's better to maintainᵉ balanceᶠ.⁵

¹ the universe holds no distinction of good and evil; nature does not reward the moral and punish the evil, it is indifferent to all life, with neither love nor hate ² the sage, like the universe, acts without favoritism; he does not cling, he does not interfere where it would imbalance, he is impartial to all; it is not apathy but impartial compassion ³ in the emptiness, movement comes; it's in this indifference that difference can be had; when one can treat all without favoritism, he can see all for who they are, and thus make movements towards them in accordance with the Dao ⁴ forcing ideas exhausts itself, leaving little room for emptiness; one's Dao is not the same as another's, it would be a fool's path to try and fit them where they will not fit ⁵ allow the Dao to flow, do not enforce rigid systems on it; force, moralization, favoritism, these things draw lines where they do not exist, and thus restrain and restrict your own path

ᵃ the word 天 (tiān) also has the meaning of "natural order", not necessarily "heaven" as a metaphysical place ᵇ the term 仁 (rén) is "benevolence", "kindness", and "humanity" so likely with the negation mark 不 (bù) it's noting a lack of, but not necessarily an antonym ᶜ the term 刍狗 (chú gǒu) is the straw dog, a ritual object to be used in offerings, treated with reverence before the ceremony and then tossed aside and trampled on after; there is no love, there is no hate, it simply is and is used as it is in its place and time ᵈ the word 穷 (qióng) means to exhaust, run out, or reach the limit of ᵉ the word 守 (shǒu) means to guard, hold, maintain ᶠ the word 中 (zhōng) means center, middle, balance, the void
